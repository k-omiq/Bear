# RT-DETR-v2 checkpoint and vertical-split check: Apocalypse 109

The official [model card](https://huggingface.co/ogkalu/comic-text-and-bubble-detector) describes RT-DETR-v2 r50vd trained at 640 pixels, with tall webtoons split vertically. We pinned the repository at `16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546` and downloaded its full `detector.onnx` (168,481,531 bytes, SHA-256 `065744e91c0594ad8663aa8b870ce3fb27222942eded5a3cc388ce23421bd195`) to this worktree's ignored `spikes/chapter-rtdetr/artifacts/models/detector.onnx`. The existing app model is `detector-v4-s_int8.onnx` (11,120,765 bytes, SHA-256 `5fe9e4f576e49d4e7e8b0e029d6d3cdc252abd4694113e1cae120e62c931ea79`). These are different exports: the full graph has dynamic image height/width and 2,747 nodes; the small graph fixes 640×640 and has 1,633 nodes. It is unsafe to describe the comparison as FP32 versus INT8 precision alone.

Both checkpoints accept `images` float32 `[N,3,640,640]` and `orig_target_sizes` int64 `[N,2]`. The full export declares dynamic image dimensions; this probe sends 640×640 to both. Outputs are `labels` int64, `boxes` float32 xyxy, and `scores` float32. We use `orig_target_sizes = [width,height]`, as the app's verified export convention requires. Classes: 0 `bubble`, 1 `text_bubble`, 2 `text_free`. Score threshold: 0.35. The pinned [processor configuration](https://huggingface.co/ogkalu/comic-text-and-bubble-detector/blob/16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546/preprocessor_config.json) specifies 640×640 bilinear resizing (`resample: 2`), RGB rescaling by 1/255, no normalization, and no padding. The existing app run used nearest-neighbor sampling; the four new runs use PIL bilinear. `halves` means two contiguous 690×800 vertical tiles, each resized to 640×640, with boxes mapped to the 690×1600 page. The model card does not specify the original training split dimensions; this is a test of a plausible split, not a claim to reproduce training exactly.

All 45 chapter pages are 690×1600 JPEGs. One ONNX Runtime CPU session was retained per run with four inference threads. Times below cover only `session.run`, excluding decoding and preprocessing. SAM-TS-L mask overlap is geometric model agreement, **not** ground-truth precision or recall; bubble boxes are excluded from the text-box overlap.

| Checkpoint / input | All boxes | Text boxes | Pages with no boxes | SAM pixels in text boxes | Total inference | Median/page |

| Small, app nearest whole | 77 | 44 | 24 | 19.26% | 4.15 s | 90.6 ms |
| Small, bilinear whole | 70 | 40 | 26 | 19.26% | 3.22 s | 69.3 ms |

| Full, bilinear whole | 83 | 49 | 21 | 19.75% | 23.95 s | 530.6 ms |
| Full, bilinear halves | 110 | 71 | 12 | 32.09% | 43.34 s | 1007.6 ms |

The full checkpoint plus vertical halves recovers many more boxes and improves mask overlap. It still leaves **13 pages with no text-class box**; 12 of those have nonempty SAM masks. Separately, 12 pages have no box of any class; 11 of those have nonempty SAM masks, while page 30 has an empty mask. Page 22 has a `bubble` box but no text-class box. Page 02 has zero boxes in all five conditions, though the SAM mask has 35,136 positive pixels and visible large lettering. Page 07 has zero whole-page boxes; the small split emits one `bubble` but no text box. The full split emits two `text_free` boxes on the top half, at `(180,621,308×179)` score 0.607 and `(3,667,484×131)` score 0.359, covering 14,433 of the page's 24,322 SAM pixels. These box results support running RT-DETR for bubble/text context in parallel with a lettering mask, but not using its text boxes as a required gate for that mask.

A [source-page audit](results/full-halves-novel-box-audit.json) matched every full-split text-class box against small-whole text-class boxes using class-agnostic IoU 0.50. Of 29 geometrically unmatched boxes, 25 visibly contain text or sound effects and four cover artwork (a balloon border, debris, rocks, or facial hatching). Only 12 boxes on 11 pages plausibly add story lettering: the other text boxes rebox already detected lettering, duplicate another full-model box, or cover end-card credits. Three are partial boxes at the y=800 tile seam, and two broad boxes merge separate effects with intervening art. This targeted visual audit does not estimate full-model precision or recall, but shows why higher box count and mask overlap alone do not establish a better default.
