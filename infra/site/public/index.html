removing text from manga, on your own machine

Download for macOS (Apple Silicon) Download for Windows (x64)

v1.0.0-beta.1 · free · GPL-3.0

macOS on apple silicon, windows x64 · models downloaded on first launch · linux builds from source

demo: understand how lama works. a chapter detect run, then text over diagonal hatching cleaned with manga-LaMa from the right-click menu ("clean with"). LaMa rebuilds the hatching lines behind the text instead of painting flat paper.

use the lightest engine that does the job: fill for flat paper, manga-LaMa (the default) for screentone, halftone and line art, FLUX.2 Klein for the rare region nothing else can reconstruct, and cloud on your own GPU account, per region, when you choose it.

  • automatic cleaning pipeline: detect speech bubbles and free text, gate to Japanese script, fit masks, route to an engine, and composite
  • every cleaned region is a non-destructive layer: re-run with a different engine, tune, or delete
  • six manual tools: auto clean, brush, shapes, AI mask brush, content-aware fill, and clone / heal
  • long-strip webtoons with virtual stitching, content-minimum splitting, and bounded-memory streaming
  • pixel-exact export: untouched pixels stay bit-identical, ICC profiles and metadata preserved
  • export to PNG, TIFF, layered PSD, mask files beside each page, or CBZ
  • completely local and offline: no account, no uploads, model weights verified by SHA-256
  • cryptographically signed auto updates

this is a beta: complex backgrounds can leave faint marks, keep your originals
